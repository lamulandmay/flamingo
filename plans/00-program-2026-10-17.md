# Flamingo: radial recents, tiles, thoughts v2, daily briefing

## Context

The user asked for five things:
1. Hold ⌘⌥ to bring up a circular menu of recently opened or edited files.
2. A "•••" control that tiles documents side by side. When you pick a layout with more tiles than open files, the new tiles get a lookup bar and quick picks chosen by meaning.
3. "Omnipotent" thoughts and better semantic recall.
4. A daily inbox that fills the screen on the first open of each day, showing what needs attention and which thoughts to handle first. It must be beautiful.
5. Full Obsidian plugin support. **Dropped from this build at your request** (see "Not in this build").

Decisions already made:
- **Radial menu:** works only inside Flamingo, so no Input Monitoring permission is needed.
- **Thoughts:** all four capabilities.
  - Actionable: kind, state, when.
  - Agentic: `@ai` replies and actions, answered by a model on this Mac.
  - Threads.
  - Thoughts on anything: folders, URLs, app selections, other thoughts.
  - Also semantic review. A thought like "review conflict between these two" is understood. If the user doesn't act on it, an overnight agent writes a TL;DR memo that appears in the next morning's inbox.
- **Daily inbox:** the window zooms to fill the screen (not a separate Space) and the inbox takes over the window. Esc or ⌘1 restores the previous frame and layout.

Ground truth from exploration:
- Two panes, hard-coded as `primary`/`secondary` in about 8 loops in `AppState`.
- No `.flagsChanged` monitor exists.
- Trail is the record of opened, saved and commented files.
- Thought ids are derived from the text, so editing a thought changes its id.
- Inbox thoughts are left out of semantic search, ⌘K and Resurface.
- The inbox is a 720×460 sheet with no ranking.
- No logic tracks the first open of a day.
- 228 uncommitted files are in the tree, and other Claude sessions edit it concurrently.

## Privacy rule (overrides everything below)

**Thoughts never leave this Mac.** Every AI feature that reads a thought runs only on-device: understanding, `@ai` replies, the night shift and its memos, echo/recall, and briefing ranking.
- Allowed models: the local llama-server (qwen3.8-27b), Ollama (gemma3:4b, qwen3-embedding), Apple on-device.
- Never allowed: Claude, Claude Code, any API key, any cloud provider.
- This is hard-coded. There is no setting, no consent prompt and no fallback. When no local model is available, the deterministic rules still work, and the AI step waits until a local model is back.
- The thought paths take an endpoint type that can only be built for `127.0.0.1` or `localhost`. Making a remote endpoint is a compile-time impossibility, not a runtime check.
- `LocalOnlyTests` run the thought paths against a `URLProtocol` that fails on any host other than loopback.
- The existing Chat can still be set to a remote provider. Its retrieval must then drop every thought passage before anything leaves the Mac. Today, human thoughts are sent with Ask's context (Retrieval.swift:157-262).

## Phase 1: Hold ⌘⌥ for a ring of recent files

### Behaviour
- **Opening:**
  - Hold exactly ⌘⌥ for 0.30 s. Two modifiers must have been pressed in from fewer; going from ⌘⌥⇧ to ⌘⌥ doesn't count.
  - Required: main window key, no sheet or modal, no mouse button down, no marked IME text, a vault open.
  - A ring opens centred on the pointer, clamped inside the window.
- **Contents:** up to 8 recent files in slots starting at 12 o'clock and going clockwise in rank order. With 4 or fewer files there are 4 bigger compass slots. The active pane's file is left out.
- **Highlighting:** pointer movement since opening picks a slot.
  - 28 pt dead zone, 4° hysteresis at slot edges.
  - A haptic `.alignment` tick on each change.
- **Releasing:**
  - Releasing ⌘ or ⌥ opens the highlighted file.
  - Releasing in the dead zone closes the ring without opening anything.
- **Cancelling:**
  - Esc closes and is consumed.
  - Any other key closes the ring with no animation, and the key passes through.
  - A mouse down closes the ring, and the click passes through.
  - Window or app resign closes it.
  - Before the 0.30 s timer fires, any key, click or scroll cancels. That keeps ⌥⌘H/N/I/L/1/3/→ working.
- **Opening a file** goes through `state.reveal(file, anchor:)`:
  - If another visible pane already shows the file, focus that pane.
  - Otherwise `jump(to:anchor:nil)`, which already restores the pane's or saved anchor (`AppState.select`, AppState.swift:1522). Trail's last anchor is used only when there is none.
- **Look:**
  - Plate: 320 pt `Squircle(radius:104)` with sidebar material, a hairline and the PanelChrome shadow.
  - Hub: 120 pt squircle showing, for the highlighted file:
    - its name in `bodyMedium`
    - its folder in `monoSmall`, tertiary
    - the relative time, a `pencil` glyph if edited, and the thought count
  - Tiles: 56 pt squircles at a 116 pt radius, holding a Quick Look thumbnail in a `ConcentricSquircle`, or a `FileGlyph`.
  - A 6 pt dot marks a file edited since you last opened it.
  - Highlight: `selection` fill plus a focus ring. No size changes.
  - Motion: `Motion.fast` fades only.
- **Accessibility and QA:** Go → Recent Files submenu, built through `NSMenuDelegate`.

### Data
- New `Sources/FlamingoTrail/TrailRecents.swift`, public and additive:
  - `RecentFile`, `RecentsTuning`, `TrailRecents.rank(_:now:limit:tuning:)` and `TrailStore.recentFiles(now:limit:tuning:)`.
  - The store method filters by date before sorting, unlike `events(since:)` at Trail.swift:343.
- Frecency score:
  - Recency: `4·2^(−age/6h)`.
  - Frequency: Σ `w(kind)·2^(−age/3d)`, with weights opened 1, saved 2, commented 2.5, linked 1.5. Repeated events within 30 minutes count once per kind.
  - Multiplied by 1.25 if edited in the last 7 days.
  - `.searched` events and paths under `.flamingo/` are ignored.
- New `Sources/Flamingo/RecentFiles.swift`, `RecentFilesModel`, a small `ObservableObject` held as `state.recents` (not `@Published` on AppState):
  - Refreshes off main, 400 ms after `TrailStore.didChangeNotification`, but only when `note.object === session.trail`. Capture's temporary stores post it too.
  - Stats each file and drops missing ones.
  - Marks a file **edited** when its disk mtime is later than its last open. This is the only use of mtime, so system churn in `~` doesn't count.
  - Pre-warms thumbnails for the top 9.
  - Fallback when the trail is empty or disabled: `openFiles`, then history. History times can't be used, because restored entries get `pushedAt = Date()` (Workspace.swift:44).

### Files
- **New `RadialRecents.swift`:**
  - `RadialHoldMachine`, a pure struct of inputs and effects, so it can be unit-tested.
  - `RadialMenuModel`.
  - `RadialRecentsController`, installed in AppDelegate after SwipeNavigator (AppDelegate.swift:57). It has one local monitor for `.flagsChanged`, `.keyDown`, mouse-down and scroll; it never consumes flagsChanged. It polls the pointer through `window.mouseLocationOutsideOfEventStream`.
- **New `RadialRecentsView.swift`:** a full-window overlay in RootLayout after the drop overlay (RootView.swift:93), with hit testing off.
- **`QAHook.swift`:** add `flags cmd opt|none`, `radial <deg>|center` and `open <relpath>`.
  - Synthesized events skip local monitors, so QA calls `RadialRecentsController.shared?.qaIngest` with a key-window override.
  - Logs `radial: arm|open|highlight|commit|cancel`.

## Phase 3: Thoughts v2, stored and understood (Core + Index)

### Format (additive; still Obsidian-readable)
- New `Sources/FlamingoCore/ThoughtMeta.swift`. `ThoughtMeta` carries:
  - `uid` (`t` + 9 Crockford base32 characters, stable across text edits)
  - `parent`
  - `kind` (idea/question/todo/intent) and `kindBy` (rules/model/user)
  - `state` (open/done/snoozed/dismissed) and `stateAt`
  - `due`, `snoozeUntil`, `modified`
  - `intent {action, objects, confidence, model, at}`
  - `work`: the memo path
  - unknown keys, round-tripped
- `Comment` gains `meta` through a new init overload. The old init is untouched.
- Only decisions are persisted. The kind and due date from the rules are recomputed from the text at index time.
- Legacy thoughts get a uid lazily, the first time something refers to them. Notes are never rewritten in bulk.
- **One metadata line** per thought, `  <!-- flamingo: {...} -->`, merging provenance, meta and extras. It **always includes `origin`**, so older builds still decode it as provenance and never leak it into the text.
  - **Bug fix:** any metadata line is metadata, never text (SidecarFormat.swift:381-388). Non-JSON lines are kept verbatim in `meta.unparsed`.
  - `normalize()` (:187) and `contentKey` (:193) must carry `meta`.
- **Threads:** replies are top-level bullets placed after the parent's last descendant, with `parent: <uid>`. No format change.
- **Selections from another app:** the selected text becomes indented `> ` quote lines.
- **URLs:** `notes/_web/<host>/<slug>-<hash6>.md` with `url:` and `title:` in the front matter. Add `FileRef.webDirectory`, `isWeb` and `isVirtual`. The `isInbox` call sites in GraphTypes, GraphFolders and Resurface switch to `isVirtual`.
- **Folders:** `notes/<folder>.md` with `subject: folder`. It can't collide: a file `x.md` maps to `x.md.md`.
- **New `SidecarStore` API:**
  - `comment(uid:in:)`, `update(uid:in:_:)`, `ensureUID(forCommentID:in:)`, `appendReply(_:toUID:in:)` and `append(_:to:frontmatter:)`.
  - All locked read-modify-write. Background writers address thoughts by uid only.
- **Rules:**
  - `ThoughtRules.read(text, at:)`:
    - question: ends in `?`, `？`, `吗` or `为什么`
    - intent: an AI-doable verb (review, compare, reconcile, summarize, check…) plus a link, an anchor or conflict wording
    - todo: an imperative opening, or need to / must / remember to
    - idea: everything else
    - It also detects `@ai` and `@flamingo`.
  - `DueParser` is anchored at the thought's own timestamp: today, tomorrow, weekdays, next week, in N days, eow, 明天, 下周. `NSDataDetector` is used only for absolute dates.
  - `ThoughtAction`: `ask`, `remind`, `connect`, `open` and `work(recipe, objects)`.
- `WorkMemo` (Core): an Obsidian Markdown memo with front matter (thought uid, recipe, verdict, sources with anchor and mtime, local model, tokens, created). Lives at `.flamingo/work/<yyyy-MM-dd>/<slug>.md`, with an append-only `work/ledger.jsonl`. Add `Vault.workDir`.

### Index v9 and recall (FlamingoIndex)
- **Schema v9** adds these `comments` columns:
  - `uid`, `parent`
  - `kind`, `kind_by`
  - `state` (default `open`)
  - `due`, `snooze_until`, `modified`
  - `origin`, `action`, `meta`
- New indexes: uid, parent, and `(state, kind, due)`.
- A `comments_reload` flag makes VaultSession reload comments once. Vectors survive, because the delete triggers copy them into `semantic_cache`.
- A metadata-only change becomes an in-place `UPDATE`. Today `replaceComments` (IndexStore.swift:833-853) deletes and reinserts, which would churn vectors.
- **Fix the drops:** change inner joins to `LEFT JOIN files` for thoughts with no file (inbox, web, folder) at Semantic.swift:336-339 and :377-381, UnifiedSearch.swift:513 and :544, and Resurface.swift:27.
- **New `ThoughtSearch.swift`:**
  - `thoughts(_ ThoughtQuery)` (SQL triage), `thread(rootUID:in:)`
  - `searchThoughts(_:inFolder:limit:includeSemantic:)`: hybrid FTS plus `VectorIndex.nearest(allow: isComment)`, fused with reciprocal rank
  - `similarThoughts(to draft:…)`: the "you thought this before" lookup while typing
  - `resurfaceableThought(near:olderThan:now:seen:)`: relevance-aware, no longer random
  - `thoughtDegree(of:)`
  - `UnifiedSearchResponse.thoughts`: thought rows in ⌘K
- **Richer embedding input:** `"<Question|To do|Intent|Thought> on <file>, <anchor> · re: <parent> · links: <titles>"` plus the body. State and due are left out, so vectors only change when the text changes.
- **GraphBuilder:** folder thoughts count on the `folder/` node. Links written by AI are counted as suggestions, not explicit links. This is a behaviour change.
- **Eval:** `SemanticEval` accepts `thought:<path>|<ts>` keys. Add about 25 cases in `fixtures/thought-cases.json` (paraphrase, Chinese and cross-language). Add the CLI flag `--eval … --mode thoughts`.
  - Targets: thought recall@5 ≥ 0.90, MRR ≥ 0.80, a ⌘K thought row in the top 3 for ≥ 85% of cases.
  - Echo: the earlier thought in the top 2 for ≥ 8 of 10 related drafts, and nothing shown for ≥ 9 of 10 unrelated drafts.
  - No file-level regression from 0.939/0.841 (fixture) and 0.990/0.955 (bilingual).

### AI (FlamingoAsk)

  - `understanding`: Ollama gemma3:4b with a JSON-schema `format`, as `Titler` does. The 27B only if it's already running.
  - `@ai` replies: the local 27B (`LlamaServer`), else Ollama.

- **`ThoughtReader.understand`:** runs about 2 s after a save and never blocks it.
  - Persists kind and intent through `update(uid:)` only when confidence ≥ 0.6 and the objects resolve.

  - Idle backfill: open thoughts from the last 30 days, at most 50 a day.
- **`@ai` / `@flamingo`:** a local-only `askStreamingLocally(files:)` with `MultiFileSource` over the target, its links and the thread's files.

  - The answer is saved through `appendReply` as `Answer.wikified()`, with citations as `[[path#anchor]]`.
- **Actions:**
  - `remind` sets `due`.
  - `connect` is only offered, with ✓.
  - `open` happens only when you trigger it.
  - `work` queues a night-shift job, or runs it now.
- **Retrieval fix** (Retrieval.swift:168, 222-225): a semantic thought hit stays a thought passage, starting with `re: <parent>`.
- **Privacy fix:** `AskEngine.run` (AskEngine.swift:159-189) filters out every `.comment` passage, thought-derived section and thought text in the history before calling any non-local provider. Covered by a test.
- **`WorkRecipes.swift`:** `review-conflict`, `compare-versions` (`FileFamilies`), `summarize`, `answer`, `find-related`.
  - Each uses anchored sections plus sections ranked from extracted index text, and returns typed JSON, e.g. `{title, tldr, verdict, conflicts[{a,b,why}], consistent, next}`.
  - The prompt treats passages as quoted data.
  - Recipes read only the files the thought names. Nothing in the output is executed.

### Night shift (new module `FlamingoThoughts`, no views; needs a `Package.swift` edit)
- **Planner:** an open intent, or a question/todo with a recipe, becomes a job when all of these hold:
  - it is ≥ 6 h old;
  - it has no human reply;
  - Trail attention on its objects since it was written is under 3 minutes (new `TrailStore.attention(on:since:)`);
  - no finished ledger job has the same fingerprint (uid:recipe:hash of the sources' path|mtime|size);
  - if the sources changed, ≥ 7 days have passed since the last memo.
- **Gate** (pure, injected clock):
  - Conditions: 22:00–06:00 local, on AC, not in Low Power, thermal state below serious, idle ≥ 15 minutes, not already run tonight.
  - A `budgetOverride` hook lets plan 13's `ComputeBudget` plug in later without merging it now.
  - Night budget: 6 jobs, 45 minutes, 150k local tokens, 8 minutes per job.
- **Scheduler:**
  - `NSBackgroundActivityScheduler` every 30 minutes, tolerance 10.
  - While working: an `IOPMAssertion` against idle sleep and `beginActivity`.
  - Idle time from `CGEventSource.secondsSinceLastEventType`.
  - Stops llama-server afterwards if it started it.
- **Runner:**
  - Writes the memo with `AtomicFile`, then an AI reply on the thought through `appendReply(toUID:)`.
  - The reply's visible text carries the TL;DR and source wikilinks, so Obsidian sees them. The memo path goes in the hidden `work` key.
  - Idempotent through the ledger; resumes after a crash, up to 2 attempts.
  - Never writes outside `.flamingo`; never records Trail events.
- **Catch-up:** if the Mac slept or the app was closed, the first open of the day runs up to 3 jobs in 12 minutes, on AC or battery ≥ 50%. Their memo cards fill in inside the briefing.
- **Palette commands:** Pause/Resume Night Shift, Work on Open Thoughts Now.

## Phase 5: The daily briefing (replaces the Capture Inbox sheet)

### Trigger
- `BriefingClock.day = startOfDay(now − 4h)`.
- Fires on whichever comes first:
  - launch, after `restoreLastVault()` at AppDelegate.swift:76 and once comments have loaded
  - `didBecomeActive`
  - the first activation after `NSWorkspace.didWake`
- Skipped when:
  - a sheet or the capture panel is up
  - the activation was an open-file request (the day stays pending)
  - there is nothing to show
  - running under XCTest
  - the "Morning Inbox" toggle is off
- Writes `Defaults.lastBriefingDay`, in a suite switchable by `FLAMINGO_DEFAULTS_SUITE` so QA never touches real settings.

### Window
- Saves the frame, full-screen state, mode, inspector state and tab.
- Animates `setFrame(screen.visibleFrame)` over 220 ms. Native full screen is left alone.
- Esc, ⌘1 or ⌘0 fades it out over 150 ms and restores everything. The frame is not restored if you moved the window meanwhile.
- Opening it later (ribbon tray at Sidebar.swift:115, palette "Inbox", ⌘0, which is free) doesn't zoom.
- It is an **overlay state** on RootLayout, not a new `FlamingoMode`. `Mode.allCases` drives ⌘1–4 and `SegmentedModes`.

### Content
- `BriefingSnapshot` is built off main. Targets: ≤ 250 ms on the fixture, ≤ 600 ms on the 896k-file `~` index. No graph build; centrality comes from SQL counts.
- **Overnight memos:** cards for memos you haven't seen.
- **Thoughts to address first:** the top 12, ranked by `AttentionRanker` (pure).
  - `score = 3.0·Due + 1.6·Kind + 0.7·Strength + 1.0·Staleness + 1.2·Activity + 0.5·Centrality + 1.5·Awaiting − 0.35·min(3, times skipped)`
  - Overdue and due-today items are pinned first.
  - Ideas show only when there's activity or something awaiting you.
  - Snoozed thoughts return when their snooze ends.
  - Each row's reason glyph comes from its strongest term.
- **Needs attention:**
  - files with open thoughts that were edited after both the thought and your last open
  - loose `_inbox` captures, with attach kept (`LinkMatcher` + `attachInboxThought`)
  - unseen similar-file suggestions for your 5 most-thought-about files
  - recovered drafts (the unused `recoverDraft()`)
  - index problems
- **Resume:** yesterday's `locationsToReopen`, up to 6.
- `BriefingHistory` in `.flamingo/briefing.json` keeps shown counts and seen memos.

### Triage keys
- j/k or ↑/↓ move. Return jumps to the thought's place, selecting the rail row by uid.
- `e` done, with a 150 ms `Color.thought` tint and `Chime.tick`.
- `s` snooze, `r` reply inline, ⌫ dismiss, `a` attach, ✓/× for suggestions, ⌘Z undo.
- Keys go through an AppKit first-responder view, so the sidebar's type-to-select doesn't take them.

### Look (within DESIGN.md)
- Centred content up to 1080 pt, on a 12-column grid with 24 pt gutters. No headers.
- **Date line:** "Saturday" in `text`, "October 17" in `textTertiary`, both in the new `Font.display` (26 pt semibold), the only display size in the product.
- **Memo cards:** 4 columns each, three across.
  - `Squircle(Radius.l)` on `bgRaised` with a hairline.
  - Up to 3 overlapping 44 pt source thumbnails, then the memo title in `title`, the TL;DR (4 lines), your thought in `small`/tertiary, and a verdict glyph.
  - Click opens `MemoReader`, a 640 pt panel rendered with `MarkdownLite`.
- **Below the cards:**
  - Thoughts to address first, 7 columns, 44 pt rows of two lines. Line 1: `KindGlyph` and the thought. Line 2: file glyph and name, anchor chip, date, reason glyph.
  - Needs attention, 5 columns of `flamingoRow`s.
  - Resume strip at the bottom: 96×64 squircle thumbnails.
  - Below 900 pt everything stacks.
- **Motion:** 220 ms fades only, with a 40 ms stagger. Nothing moves.
